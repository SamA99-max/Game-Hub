# Ursina-games
All these games are created using one graphics engine called ursina this originally was to test out how good I am in Python, but like this game_hub project (i like to call it) where i created like a small game hub myself and recreated some iconic games just without those huge file sizes and extra permissions ;)

Make sure to have the library: ursina and version: 7.0.0
And install the folder as-it-is and run the main.py where you'll find a "blueprint" on where which file should be. (These files are sorted in the order I made them not in the alphabetic order)

**Chess**

You can click each piece to see where it can move this game doesn't have en passent or promotion to any other piece than a queen (just take the best piece and go :3)
you can move with W,A,S,D,C,V,U,O,Q,E with B you can go to the Blue side and with H to the Red side with R you can go to the default camera position
Checks and check mates will be highlighted and draw doesn't exist ( in any Form)
****

**Snake**

This is the Snake Xenzia game from Nokia
you can select your snakes speed at the beginning with 1 being the slowest and 5 the highest (select with your keyboard)
with all arrows you can move in all directions and eat the apples ( a red pixel)
****

**Tic** **Tac** **Toe**

I think pretty self-explanatory; you play against a bot 
****

**Pong**

inspired by the first ever Video-Game ever.
Firstly select (with your keyboard) 1 for single player and 2 for multiplayer if you select 1 choose the difficulty to 1-5 (also with your keyboard)
play with arrow up and arrow down (single player and the left paddle in multiplayer) and W,S keys (in multiplayer for the right paddle)
****

**Parkour < Easy Parkour**

A Endless parkour where you move with arrows ( if  you fall off you will re-spawn at the first platform)
****

**Parkour < Hard Parkour**

Another endless parkour with moving platforms and coins where you can move with arrows (if you fall of you re-spawn at the firs platform) but at some platforms 
the score counter glitches so please use the coins counter as reference (sorry).
****

**Parkour < Gd Parkour(extreme hard and 2D)**

I had no idea for the name sorry, anyway this is another parkour where you will move to the left (automatically) and have to jump over spikes
and sometimes there are pink boosters, which launch you up.
You jump with Space, Arrow up, Left click (you can also double jump) and dash with shift (and a cooldown).
****

**Space Shooter**

This is a game where enemies come from above and bump into you to do damage also there are kamikaze enemies which launch themselves into you 
and also sniper enemies which can shoot you, also occasionally a Boss spawns.
You move with a,d, right arrow, left arrow and deploy your shield with S and shoot with Space and use heals with H.
****

**Breakout**

This is the classic Breakout game where you rebound a ball and make it destroy the tiles.
Instructions are given on screen.
****

**Minecraft < Minecraft**

A very very Extremely stripped down and almost barren version of Minecraft.
Instructions are given on screen.
****

**Minecraft < infinite Minecraft**
A Minecraft but infinite and flat with no enemies.
Movement is same to Minecraft which means: W,A,S,D - move, R - run, Space - jump, Left click - place, Right click - break and world gets automatically saved.
****

**Tetris**
I mean its just Tetris but bigger.
Right arrow and Left arrow to move and Space to Rotate.
